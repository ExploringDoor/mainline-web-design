Straight Answers

What commissioners ask before they say yes

Can we actually run it ourselves?

Yes. Score entry, schedule changes, postponements, and news are all built for volunteers on a phone. Try the admin in the demo; that's the real workflow.

We're on SportsConnect or LeagueLineup. What moves?

Scores, schedules, standings, brackets, team pages, and communication move to your new site. If you're a Little League that has to keep SportsConnect for registration, keep it; PA District 27 does exactly that and links the two.

What happens if you get hit by a bus?

Your league owns the domain, the hosting account, and the data. The site is plain HTML and JavaScript on Firebase, not a proprietary platform, so any developer can pick it up. No lock-in is part of the deal.

Who handles the money?

You do. Registration runs through your own Stripe or Square account and lands in your bank. I never touch it and never take a percentage, which is why there's no per-player fee.

How long does it take?

Two to eight weeks depending on the tier, and I work backward from your registration open date or opening day. Most leagues start 8 to 10 weeks before the season.

Do parents need an app?

No. Everything is mobile web, so a texted link just works. Optional text alerts for finals, postponements, and bracket updates go out from the admin with one tap.
