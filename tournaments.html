01 Registration and payments

Teams sign up and pay. No spreadsheet.

Coaches register online, pick their events, pay by card and land in your admin with a roster attached. The money goes into your own Square or Stripe account. I never hold it and never take a cut.

Your payment account
Square or Stripe, deposited to your bank on its normal schedule. There is no per-team fee from me.
Your prices, per event
Every event carries its own entry fee. Teams that still owe get a reminder email.
Waivers and insurance
Coaches accept the waiver as they register, and teams can buy insurance right on the site.
Rosters with a deadline
Coaches manage their own rosters until the lock time you set, then the roster freezes. You can still make changes from the admin.
Who's coming
Every event shows the teams already entered, so the next coach knows the field is real.
02 Schedules and brackets

Pool play in. Champion out.

Build the schedule once. As scores post, standings update, the bracket fills in, winners move on by themselves, and the champion banner goes up the moment the final is entered.

A real bracket from Texas Select's Halloween Bash in Pleasanton, TX. Every winner advanced on its own and the champion banner went up with the final score.

Brackets that advance
"Winner of Game 2" becomes the real team as soon as the score is saved.
Release it when it's right
Build a division's schedule privately, then publish it one division at a time.
Every field, every complex
Events spread across several complexes show the right venue and directions on every game.
Print the bracket
One button prints a clean bracket for the wall at the complex.
Hotels
Each event carries its own hotel or stay-to-play link.
03 When the schedule changes

A game moves. Every coach in it hears about it.

Coaches screenshot the schedule on Friday night. A field changes Saturday morning and they show up at the old time. Now, the moment you save a change, every affected team gets an email and the public schedule flags the game.

Automatic
Nothing to remember. Save the change and the email goes out.
One email per team
Move ten games at once and each coach still gets one clean message, not ten.
Quiet until it's public
Changes to a schedule you have not released yet stay silent.
A flag they can't miss
Moved games carry a Time Changed badge on the public schedule for 48 hours.

Built for a real weekend. A director asked for this in the middle of his Halloween tournament in October. It went live that same weekend.

04 Scores by text

Coaches text the final. It posts itself.

No app and no login. A coach texts the score to your tournament's number and it lands on the site, the standings and the bracket. The coach gets a confirmation with a link back.

Two coaches, one score
The first report posts. If the other coach texts a different score, it comes down and you get an email to settle it.
Your score wins
Anything you enter in the admin outranks a text.
Undo
Fat thumbs happen. A coach can reply UNDO within an hour.
Only your coaches
A number you have not added gets a polite reply to ask the tournament director.
Ask it anything
Coaches can text NEXT, SCHEDULE or STANDINGS and get the answer back.
Registered properly
Carrier registration for business texting is handled for you.

Scoped logins

Email their own coaches
Directors can email every coach in their events. The server checks every address, so nobody can email another director's teams.
You see everything
Your login sees every event, every payment and every director.
