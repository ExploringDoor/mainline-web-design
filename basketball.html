01 Tournaments and events

Registration to champion. One site.

Teams register and pay, pools fill in, brackets advance as scores post, and every coach and parent finds their court and tip-off time from a phone.

Registration and payment
Teams pay by card into your own Square or Stripe account. No per-team fee from me.
Pools and brackets
Pool play into Gold and Silver brackets that advance on their own.
Every event, one calendar
Each event with its own page, gyms, entry fee and countdown to tip-off.
College coaches
Certification rules, a coach packet for every event, and schedules filtered by graduation year and gym.
Hotels
Stay-to-play and hotel details right next to the schedule.
02 Club teams

Coaches run their teams. The club sees everything.

Every coach gets a dashboard for their own team. Families get one code, no password. The club office stops being the middleman for every practice change.

Tennessee Flight's club page. Each team has its own schedule, roster and practices, and families open their team with one code from the coach.

The coach's own team
Roster with positions, heights and two parent contacts, plus the schedule, scores and weekly practices.
Who's coming
Families tap Going or Can't make it. The coach sees every name before the bus leaves.
The message writes itself
Move a practice or change a gym and the site writes the note to families. The coach just taps send.
One code for families
No account, no password. Add it to the home screen and it opens like an app.
Dues and fees
Coaches see paid or not yet for dues, uniforms and tryout fees, player by player.
Private stays private
Parent phone numbers and emails never appear on a public page.
