01 Scores by text

Coaches text the score. The league updates itself.

Nobody babysits a laptop on Sunday night. A coach texts the final from the dugout and the score, standings, team pages and recap all update before the field is raked.

No app, no login
Coaches text a number. That is the whole job.
Two coaches, one score
The first report posts. If the other coach texts a different score, it comes down and the league office gets an email to settle it.
The office wins
Anything entered in the admin outranks a text.
Undo
A coach can reply UNDO within an hour.
Only your coaches
Numbers the league has not added are politely turned away.

02 Registration and payments

Registration that pays you. Not a platform.

Player and team registration, tryouts and waivers, paid by card straight into your league's own Square or Stripe account.

No per-player fee
Nothing from me, and no "platform fee" line for parents at checkout. Your processor takes its normal card fee and that is it.
Teams, players, tryouts
Each with its own form, price and deadline.
Waivers built in
Parents sign the waiver as part of registering, not on a clipboard at the field.
One list for the board
Every registration and payment in one admin, so the treasurer is not reconciling three spreadsheets.
03 Camps, clinics and the shop

The office posts the clinic. The site sells it.

Island Fastpitch's office posts its own camps and clinics: the date, the sessions, the prices, the player limits and the flyer. The site builds the page, takes the card, counts the places and closes each session when it fills.

Sessions with their own limits
A full morning session leaves the afternoon open.
Bundle pricing
One session, two or all three, each at its own price.
No waiting on me
Island's office has published two clinics this fall on its own, flyers and all.
Team shop
Spirit wear and gear sold right on the league site.
