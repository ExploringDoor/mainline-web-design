What happens when you hit Publish: the game moves to Final, the ticker rebuilds, standings recompute (W/L, GB, diff, streak), and the playoff seeds re-rank. On a real site this also fires the push notification and the team text alert.
What you just used
01 · Live scoring

Game state, not a text field

Innings, outs, runners, and runs tracked as real game state. When a game ends it finalizes itself and feeds everything downstream.

02 · Auto standings

Nobody types a record

W, L, PCT, games back, run differential, streaks, and last five are derived from results every time. Tiebreakers are rules, not opinions.

03 · Bracket engine

Seeds that follow the season

Playoff matchups are generated from the standings. Single or double elimination, pool play to bracket, and if-necessary games are all supported.

04 · Admin portal

Built for commissioners

Score entry, postponements, schedule changes, and publish-with-undo. Your managers do it from their phone in the parking lot.
